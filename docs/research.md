# Selection and source notes

Checked 15 November 2026. New family: service-queue-operations. This is the staffed-counter queue and its service history, distinct from building security sign-in, appointments, learning operations and freight rates.

Qminder Premium lists US$12,588 annually, or US$1,149 monthly, including 50 users. Business lists US$9,468 annually or US$869 monthly. The vendor page's structured pricing data labels the currency USD. These are public list prices, not verified customer invoices. Annual offers are used as printed, not inferred from a percentage banner.

Scores in order: annual bill 2, data-shaped 5, our buyers pay 1, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 4. Total 18/30. No evidence of Qminder use was found in the available customer brains or CRM snapshots; live CRM was not read. Search results include comparison pages and review directories, but no measured search volume was available. Reviews are generally positive; this build makes no claim that customers dislike the incumbent.

TitleLedger scored 15/30: its enterprise starting price lacks a billing period, so no annual bill was inferred. MovieChainer scored 14/30: its available public price evidence was historical. Ziflow was rejected because visual proof review needs a front end. Existing learning and freight families were excluded.

## Scope of this build

The local base supports staffed arrivals and service records, seven business record types, read-only reports, two document types, privacy evidence checks and a documented export import. Synthetic workbooks exercise the documented Ticket List shape. A real customer export and a staffed-counter trial remain necessary before a production switch. Runtime performance, security certifications, SMS delivery and hardware compatibility have not been claimed.
